## E) Reality Check

- **Luxury brands (LVMH, Kering, Richemont, Chanel, Hermès) are NOT aggressive US AI hirers.** Most group-level AI/ML hiring happens in Europe (Paris/Milan/Geneva). Their US roles are dominated by retail, marketing, and corporate functions.
- **The real AI/ML fashion opportunities in the US are at fashion-TECH companies** — StitchFix, The RealReal, Rent the Runway, Farfetch, and LVMH's tech subsidiaries (Sephora Beauty Tech, 24S).
- **Ralph Lauren is the outlier among traditional luxury** — they have a visible US AI investment (Ask Ralph) and hire in NYC.
- **Expect a compensation drop vs. pure-tech roles.** Luxury brands typically pay 20–30% below FAANG/AI-labs for equivalent levels. Fashion-tech (StitchFix, RealReal) is closer to tech comp.
